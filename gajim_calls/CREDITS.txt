Gajim Calls - Third-Party Credits
=================================

Built-in ringtones
------------------
The bundled ringtones 089, 090, and 091 were created by universfield and
sourced from Pixabay. The original uploads supplied for this plugin were:

- universfield-ringtone-089-496413.mp3
- universfield-ringtone-090-496416.mp3
- universfield-ringtone-091-496417.mp3

The plugin ships size-optimized Opus-in-Ogg conversions of those audio files.
Ringtone 090 is the default selection. The original audio files are not
redistributed in the plugin archive.
